# SMART PORTS CO.LTD — UI Design System

## Brand
One unified enterprise technology partner formed through the strategic partnership of Dynamic Company and Smart Ports. The experience communicates operational intelligence, trust, integration, and global scale.

## Color tokens
- Primary / Deep Navy: `#0B1F3A`
- Secondary / Smart Blue: `#1E5EFF`
- Accent / Cyan Tech: `#00D4FF`
- Light background: `#F5F7FA`
- Dark background: `#0A0F1C`
- Primary text: `#111827`
- Secondary text: `#6B7280`
- Success: `#22C55E`
- Warning: `#F59E0B`
- Error: `#EF4444`
- Borders: `#DDE4EE`

## Typography
- Arabic: Cairo with IBM Plex Sans Arabic fallback.
- English: Inter.
- Display H1: responsive 42–64px, 800 weight, tight tracking.
- H2: responsive 32–45px, 800 weight.
- H3: 17–23px, 700–800 weight.
- Body: 13–16px, 400–500 weight, 1.8–2 line height for Arabic.
- Labels: 9–12px, 700 weight.

## Layout & spacing
- 12-column enterprise grid principles, responsive content max-width 1200px.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 88px.
- Standard card radius: 12–18px.
- Borders: low-contrast cool gray; dark surfaces use subtle blue-white borders.
- Responsive breakpoints: 1050px, 850px, 560px.
- RTL Arabic is default. Language toggle switches document direction to LTR for English.

## Components
- Header with announcement strip, sticky navigation, language toggle, primary CTA.
- Buttons: primary, ghost, white, small.
- Eyebrow labels and accent dots.
- Solution cards, metric cards, industry rows, detail panels.
- Dark hero/data visualizations, CTA banner, footer.
- Contact form fields with focus and validation states.
- Responsive mobile navigation.
- Reduced-motion preference support.

## Pages
- `index.html`: home / company overview.
- `solutions.html`: five solution families.
- `industries.html`: industry-specific applications.
- `about.html`: partnership, values, delivery approach.
- `insights.html`: editorial thought-leadership cards.
- `contact.html`: contact details and demo form.

## Production checklist
1. Replace the placeholder email `hello@smartports.example` with the company mailbox.
2. Connect the contact form to a backend, CRM, or secure form service.
3. Add verified corporate address, phone, privacy policy, and legal terms.
4. Confirm all translated content with a professional Arabic/English reviewer.
5. Run accessibility, browser, and device testing before launch.
